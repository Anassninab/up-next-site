Up Next (A4S_Upload)

Up Next is a small desktop application that runs on the creator's own computer. It helps one creator keep a queue of their own finished short videos, get reminders, and upload those videos to their own YouTube and TikTok accounts through the platforms' official APIs.

  • Sign in to your own TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit.
  • Send your own videos to your TikTok drafts, or post them after you review the caption, visibility and settings.
  • Everything runs locally on your computer. Nothing is uploaded to any server owned by the app.
