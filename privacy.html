Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Up Next (A4S_Upload) is a desktop application that runs entirely on your own computer. The author of the app does not operate any server that receives your data.

What the app uses

  • TikTok sign-in. When you connect TikTok, the app receives an access token and a refresh token for your account, and your TikTok display name, username and the posting options TikTok allows for your account.
  • Your videos and text. The video files, titles, captions and hashtags you choose are sent directly from your computer to TikTok (or YouTube) when you ask the app to upload them.

Where it is stored

Tokens, settings and your queue are stored only in a folder on your own computer. They are not sent to the author or to any third party other than TikTok (and YouTube, if you connect it) for the purpose of posting your videos.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell, share or analyse your personal data, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Your control

You can disconnect TikTok at any time from the app, which revokes the token and deletes it from your computer. You can also remove the app's access in TikTok under Settings, Security and permissions, Apps and websites. Deleting the app's data folder removes everything it stored.
